Capabilities

Every piece of the handling chain, engineered as one system.

Whichever part of the line you need covered — a single hoist or a fully-integrated manipulator cell — we specify, source and support it against your duty cycle and UK compliance requirements.

Hoists, Grippers & Tooling

Lifting equipment matched to your duty cycle

Chain and wire hoists, application-specific grippers and end-of-arm tooling — specified for safe working load, headroom, cycle speed and product protection, not picked from a generic catalogue.

  • Chain & wire rope hoists sized to your safe working load
  • Application-specific grippers & vacuum/mechanical tooling
  • Full certification pack from installation, not retrofitted

Chain Hoists

Compact, low-headroom electric chain hoists for confined workstations.

Wire Rope Hoists

Higher-capacity hoisting for heavy or continuous-duty applications.

Vacuum Tooling

Suction-based end-effectors for panels, boards and delicate surfaces.

Mechanical Grippers

Custom-jawed grippers engineered around your product geometry.

Gantry & Overhead Cranes

Structural lifting, engineered to span your building

Fixed and travelling gantry cranes engineered up to 80 metres span and 20 tonnes capacity — integrated into new builds or retrofitted into existing structures with minimal disruption.

  • Single and double-girder overhead travelling cranes
  • Free-standing gantry systems for sites without structural steel
  • Full structural, electrical and controls integration

Intelligent Manipulators

Pneumatic manipulators, backed by AOE in the UK

For precise, fatigue-free product handling — including fully line-tracking cells — we design integrated manipulator solutions and are the UK supply & service partner for the ATIS pneumatic manipulator range, manufactured in Italy with over 30 years of engineering behind it.

ATISferax

Lightweight, compact and responsive manipulators designed for restricted working areas and intensive cycle times.

Articulated Arm Manipulators

Rigid arm with parallelogram vertical movement, built for precise handling of offset loads or product rotation.

Steel Cable Manipulators

Twin steel-cable vertical excursion for rapid, balanced handling where speed matters most.

Weight Balancers

Compressed-air cylinder balancing that neutralises product weight the instant the operator takes hold.

Column & Ceiling Mounting

Column, ceiling or rail mounting on floor or overhead track, to suit your existing structure.

Mobile & Self-Stabilising Bases

Wheeled, pallet-truck-mounted or self-stabilising base options for flexible, movable installations.
