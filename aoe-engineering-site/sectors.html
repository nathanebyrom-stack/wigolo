Sectors We Serve

Handling equipment shaped by the sector it serves.

Every industry moves product differently. We specify equipment against the constraints of your sector — not a one-size-fits-all catalogue.

Automotive

Body-in-white manipulators, powertrain handling, and line-tracking manipulator cells engineered around fixed takt times, repeatable tolerances and high-cycle reliability.

Aerospace & Defence

Process handling equipment for military and civil aerospace manufacturing — engineered to exacting tolerance, traceability and documentation requirements.

Food & Beverage

Hygienic, wash-down-capable handling solutions for high-throughput lines, including balancers and manipulators suited to variable product weights.

Pharmaceutical & Life Sciences

Precise, traceable handling for controlled environments, engineered to minimise contamination risk and support validated processes.

Logistics & Warehousing

Mobile and self-stabilising manipulators, wheeled bases and pallet-truck-mounted equipment for flexible, multi-point handling.

General Manufacturing

Hoists, grippers and gantry cranes specified against the duty cycle, headroom and safe working load of your specific process.
