Our Story

From Andron Handling Solutions to AOE Engineering

AOE Engineering Ltd was established in Burnham, Berkshire, when founders Gary Adams and David Ogden renamed Andron Handling Solutions Ltd to reflect a broader ambition: not just to supply handling equipment, but to engineer and manage the full solution around it.

Since then, we've delivered more than £60 million of equipment into UK and international manufacturing — from bespoke automotive body-in-white manipulators to gantry cranes spanning 80 metres and military and civil aerospace process equipment. Every project runs on the same principle: bring together the right design, the right partners, and the right aftercare, and manage the risk out of the programme.

That principle is why we operate as the UK supply and service partner for ATIS pneumatic manipulators — rather than build every product ourselves, we back the equipment that best suits your process, and stand behind it with our own engineering and aftercare team.
