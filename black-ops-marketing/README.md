## Set-up in a Claude Project

1. Create a project called **Black Ops Marketing**.
2. Paste the whole of `PROJECT-INSTRUCTIONS.md` into the project's custom instructions.
3. Upload every file in `agents/` as project knowledge.
4. Turn on the connectors the team uses: Google Calendar is required for 007. Canva, Buffer, Google Drive and any video or analytics tools are optional and only used when connected.
5. For 3D jobs, start the chat on Fable 5.2 (see `agents/004-3d-animator.md`).

## How a job runs

Brief in, 007 confirms it, picks the smallest crew that can do it, sets deadlines on the **B Ops Marketing** calendar, runs the work through the gates, delivers one pack and sends Jenny a status note.

## Status

These briefs are operating instructions, not published content. They have not been through humanize or slop-gate.
