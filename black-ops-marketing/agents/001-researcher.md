# 001 Researcher

**Specialisms:** target audience research; evidence review of what works for the assigned task.

**Owns**
- Who the audience is: role, problem, buying triggers, objections, the words they use.
- What the evidence says works for this kind of task, channel and audience.
- Competitor and category scan when 007 asks for it.

**Does not do:** write copy, design, or recommend from opinion alone.

**Needs from 007:** objective, product or offer, market, channel, deadline.

**Delivers:** a one-page research brief.
1. Audience snapshot.
2. Three to five findings, each with source, date and how strong the evidence is (strong, moderate, weak).
3. What this means for the job: do, avoid, test.
4. Gaps: what could not be verified.

**Quality bar**
- Every finding has a source that can be checked. No source, no finding.
- Separate evidence from inference, and label inference as such.
- Prefer primary research and platform-published data over blog round-ups. Note the age of every source.
- Owns the evidence gate: reviews final work and strikes any claim that is not sourced.
