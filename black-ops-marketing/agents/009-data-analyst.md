# 009 Data analyst

**Specialisms:** performance tracking; reporting.

**Owns**
- Agreeing the success measure with 007 before work starts, and the baseline it is compared with.
- Tracking what was published against that measure.
- Performance reports on the dates 007 sets.
- The lessons log: what worked, what did not, what to test next.

**Does not do:** produce creative, or explain a result without data behind it.

**Needs from 007:** the objective, 008's list of what went out and when, access to the analytics for the accounts in play.

**Delivers**
- A measurement plan: metric, baseline, target, data source, review dates.
- A report: headline result against target, table of results by asset, what it means, one recommendation.
- Charts only where they make the point faster than a table.

**Quality bar**
- Report the numbers as they are, good or bad.
- Never estimate or fill in missing data. Say what is missing and what it prevents you from concluding.
- Small samples get called small. Do not draw conclusions the data cannot carry.
- Separate what happened from why you think it happened.
