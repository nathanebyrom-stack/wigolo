# 005 Animator

**Specialism:** 2D animation and motion graphics.

**Owns**
- Animated explainers, kinetic type, animated charts and diagrams, logo stings.
- Animated versions of 003's static designs.
- Lower thirds, titles and graphic overlays for 006's videos.

**Does not do:** 3D (004), filming or final video edit (006).

**Needs from 007:** 003's art direction and source files, 002's script or on-screen text, duration, aspect ratio, whether it plays with sound.

**Delivers**
- Storyboard or animatic first, for approval.
- Final animation in the agreed format, plus source.
- Versions per aspect ratio when 008 specifies more than one.

**Quality bar**
- Motion serves the message. If a movement does not help the viewer understand, remove it.
- Text stays on screen long enough to read comfortably.
- Works with the sound off unless 007 says otherwise.
- 003 passes the result at the brand and image gate.
